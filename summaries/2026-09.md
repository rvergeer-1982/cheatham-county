## Ashland City — Mayor and Council

Two files, two different meeting types, at two different stages of completeness:

### September 1, 2026 — Regularly Scheduled Workshop Meeting (approved minutes)

This document is titled "Minutes" and reads as a completed record: it has roll call, recorded motions with seconds and voice-vote results, and a signature block (unsigned in this copy) for the Mayor and CMFO. Workshop meetings appear to function as a preview/discussion session ahead of the regular council meeting a week later — most items here are simply staff/department heads explaining what a matter is, with no vote taken except on procedural items (approving the agenda, approving prior minutes, adjournment).

**What happened / what's on the table:**
- Roll call: Mayor Greer and Councilmen Adkins, Kerrigan, Smith, Thompson, and Young present; Councilwoman Binkley absent.
- Agenda approved with changes: department head reports and item #16 removed; item #29 clarified to mean Phase 1 (see flag below).
- August 4, 2026 workshop minutes approved.
- No public forum comments.
- **Budget/finance items discussed** (no votes at this workshop stage, no dollar figures given in the minutes):
  - Ordinance 653, Budget Amendment #1 FY27 (1st reading) — described as funding a donation to the police department and covering change orders for work at John's Park.
  - Ordinance 655, Budget Amendment #2 FY27 — described as reappropriating 2026 road paving funds into 2027, plus additional funding for the Bicentennial Trail.
  - CEC Settlement Agreement — described as CEC (a contractor/vendor) agreeing to pay the town to settle over leaks at the sewer plant. No dollar amount given.
  - Leak Repair at Sewer Plant — a quote CEC obtained for repairing the leaks referenced above.
  - Award of paving bid to TVP (described as the lowest bidder; amount not given).
  - Award of Bicentennial Trail bid to Adams, out of two bids received (amount not given).
  - Request for additional funding for the Bicentennial Trail — see flag below on Phase 1 vs. Phase 2 confusion.
- **Contracts/agreements discussed**: Karda Agreement (police department cameras/RMS system), Mid-Cumberland Agreement (transportation for members to a center), TACN Contract (radio system tie-in with Motorola), Waste Water Line Easement Agreement (shared line with AO Smith), Consulting Agreement (Margie Jerrell, to complete property taxes this year), Water and Ice Purification System Agreement (fire department station), Clarksville Arts Commission Grant (contract for an arts festival), a presentation from Dennis LaMotte on something called "Switcheroo" (no further detail given in the minutes).
- **Personnel/administrative**: Resolution 2026-45 updates the Paytable and City Recorder job description, described as being done in order to hire for that position — suggests a vacancy is being filled. Resolution 2026-46 amends live-stream meeting policy to add the Parks Board to Zoom meetings.
- **Ordinances discussed**: Ordinance 654 (development-fee justification required by state Public Chapter 140), Ordinance 656 (repeat code offenders), Ordinance 657 (updating the burn permit ordinance).
- Fire department surplus: five items nominated for surplus (not itemized in this document).
- Meeting adjourned 7:24 p.m.

**Flags / issues:**
- Item #29 is titled "Request Additional Funding for Bicentennial Trail **Phase 2**" in the minutes, but the Mayor's own clarifying note during agenda approval says "Item #29 is for Phase 1," and the discussion text under that item also refers to "additional funding of phase 1." This is an internal inconsistency in the minutes themselves (title says Phase 2, both spoken clarifications say Phase 1) — worth confirming which phase is actually being funded when the regular meeting's minutes are available.
- Item #32 was listed as "Resolution 2026-47: Public Chapter 140," but the Town Attorney (Ms. Noe) clarified during the meeting that this is actually being handled as an ordinance (Ordinance 654) and that Resolution 2026-47 does not exist. This is self-corrected within the document, not a discrepancy between documents.
- No dollar figures are given anywhere in these minutes for any of the budget amendments, the CEC settlement, or either bid award (paving, Bicentennial Trail) — the magnitude of these financial actions cannot be verified from this document alone.
- Nothing here suggests wrongdoing; these read as normal routine agenda items for a small-town workshop meeting.
