# Cheatham County, TN — Public Boards & Commissions Reference

Compiled 2026-09-09. This is a starting inventory of public boards, committees, and commissions operating at the county and municipal level in Cheatham County, TN, with links to their official web presence. Meeting schedules and member rosters change — verify against the source before relying on any date or name here.

**Note on sourcing:** County-level board/committee details below come from two PDFs linked off the county commission page: [County Boards and other Committee Meeting Schedules](https://www.cheathamcountytn.gov/assets/pdfs/county_boards.pdf) (updated 12/12/18, plus embedded member rosters updated as recently as 01/2026) and [County Standing Committees](https://www.cheathamcountytn.gov/assets/pdfs/committees.pdf) (as of 12/16/2025). These are the county's own documents but carry mixed "last updated" dates — treat member names/terms as needing reconfirmation, not gospel.

## Nashville Electric Service (NES)

Not a Cheatham County or municipal governmental body — NES is the electric utility serving Nashville and parts of the surrounding area, including power service in Cheatham County. Added 2026-09-11 at Rob's request to track alongside the county/city boards.

Main site: [nespower.com](https://www.nespower.com/)

| Board / Committee | Website |
|---|---|
| Electric Power Board | [Our Board](https://www.nespower.com/our-board/#be992d8d-1f69-4847-92ef-18c880180896) |
| Audit & Ethics Committee | [Our Board](https://www.nespower.com/our-board/#be992d8d-1f69-4847-92ef-18c880180896) |

**Sourcing note:** Minutes are posted as direct PDF links on the Our Board page (pattern: `nespower.com/-/media/project/nes/common/pdfs/about-us/[year]-drafts-agendas-and-meeting-materials/[filename].pdf`) — confirmed 2026-09-11 to be directly downloadable, unlike BOEconnect's JS-gated viewer. Filenames are inconsistent (e.g., "power-board-minutes---july-2026.pdf", "signed-legal-minutes-with-appendix-a--june-24-2026.pdf") so each month's exact link should be located on the page rather than guessed from a pattern.
